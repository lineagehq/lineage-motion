## Adversarial Proof Standard

The final audit must state the user-facing claim and challenge these three
realistic failure modes with direct evidence:

1. One or more private aliases, animation applications, unsupported constructs, corrections, fonts, or ledgers are missing while the aggregate receipt still looks complete.
2. Import candidates leak into canonical documents or affect compiler, preview, service, browser, or receipt output without an explicit typed authoring operation; repeated exports may also drift.
3. Visual proof misses a discrete boundary, browser-native playback diverges from imported behavior, or private content and local details leak into tracked artifacts or sanitized receipts.

Evidence must include a complete sanitized five-alias inventory; explicit
supported, inert, deferred, and unsupported counts; repeated canonical/export
digests; compiler-native installed-Chrome traces; stable samples and both sides
of every discrete transition exercised by the slice; candidate noncanonical
checks at each boundary; repository/diff/privacy scans; and focused plus
regression test results.
